# Features

Penqwin ERP is pharmacy management software for India. This is an overview of what the Windows application covers, by menu.

## Sales

- Point of sale with batch and expiry control, multiple bills in progress, held bills and barcode scanning
- Quotations, delivery challans, and orders with delivery
- Invoices with several printable designs; sales returns and credit notes
- Payments received and receivables ageing
- Profit and margin on each invoice, shown on screen only and never printed

## Parties

- Customers with groups and categories, membership, loyalty programme, birthdays and anniversaries
- Refill desk: reminders for customers who are due to repeat a medicine
- Credit accounts and insurance or corporate claims
- Suppliers with a scorecard; staff
- Spend, profit and margin per customer, for all time, a financial year or any date range

## Purchase

- Purchase orders, goods receipt, purchase bills, supplier payments and purchase returns

## Inventory

- Products with a medicine directory and search by name or composition
- Stock by batch, expiry control, low stock and minimum and maximum levels
- Stock adjustments, physical verification, warehouses and godowns, batch and expiry-stock transfers
- Racks and shelves, barcode labels and shelf price tags

## Accounts

- Day book and day close report
- Bank accounts, bank reconciliation and cheques
- Vouchers, expenses and account ledgers
- Financial reports, cash flow and financial years

## Tax

- GST filing and GSTR-2B reconciliation
- E-invoice (IRN) and GST tax slabs

## Reports

- Product performance, batch-wise profit, fast moving, slow moving and dead stock
- ABC analysis, reorder suggestions and expiry write-off valuation

## Masters

- Ledgers, manufacturers, molecules and salts, taxes and HSN defaults, all created in place

## Compliance

- Schedule H1 and Schedule X registers
- Doctors and drug inspector visits

## Printing

- A4, A5, thermal 58 mm and 80 mm, and dot-matrix output
- Silent printing to the printer you choose, with no print dialog
- Optional PTR column on invoices

## Data and backup

- Records are stored on your own computer
- Backup and restore, and optional backup to your own Google Drive
- Import of existing data, and AI-assisted reading of purchase invoices
